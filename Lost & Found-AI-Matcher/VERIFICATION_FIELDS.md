# Verification Question Design Guide

Use this guide when entering `private_details` for found items. Each field should be easy for the real owner to answer but hard for a random person to guess.

---

### keys
- `keychain_colour` → "What colour is the keychain?"
- `number_of_keys` → "How many keys are on the ring?"
- `tag_text` → "What text, logo, or hostel name is printed on the key tag?"
- `room_or_locker_number` → "What room or locker number does the key open?"

*Example: "Red Peradeniya key tag with Room 204 written on it."*

---

### electronics
- `lock_screen_wallpaper` → "What image or colour is the lock screen wallpaper?"
- `case_colour_or_sticker` → "What colour is the case, or what sticker is on it?"
- `brand_and_model` → "What is the exact brand and model?"
- `accessory_detail` → "What charger or accessory is attached?"

*Example: "Black Samsung phone with a Moratuwa Faculty sticker on the back."*

---

### bag
- `bag_colour` → "What is the main colour of the bag?"
- `brand_or_logo` → "What brand logo or faculty logo is on the bag?"
- `contents_inside` → "Name one item clearly visible inside the bag."
- `zipper_or_patch_detail` → "Describe a unique zipper, patch, or charm on the bag."

*Example: "Blue backpack with a J'pura Science Faculty patch and a water bottle inside."*

---

### wallet
- `wallet_colour` → "What colour is the wallet?"
- `id_card_type` → "What type of ID card is inside (student ID, NIC, driving licence)?"
- `cash_note_count` → "How many notes or what denomination is visible?"
- `photo_inside` → "Describe the photo inside the wallet, if any."

*Example: "Brown leather wallet with a University of Kelaniya student ID inside."*

---

### jewellery
- `jewellery_type` → "What type of jewellery is it (ring, chain, bracelet, earring)?"
- `metal_or_colour` → "What metal or colour is it (gold, silver, rose gold)?"
- `engraving_or_initial` → "What engraving, initial, or symbol is on it?"
- `charm_shape` → "Describe the shape of any pendant or charm."

*Example: "Silver chain with a small Om pendant and the initials 'S.D.' engraved."*

---

### clothing
- `clothing_type` → "What type of clothing is it (t-shirt, jacket, saree, cap)?"
- `colour_and_pattern` → "What colour and pattern does it have?"
- `brand_or_size_label` → "What brand or size is printed on the label?"
- `unique_mark` → "Is there a stain, tear, badge, or hostel name on it?"

*Example: "Black hoodie with 'Ruhuna Engineering' printed on the back, size L."*

---

### document
- `document_type` → "What type of document is it (exam admission, assignment, certificate)?"
- `owner_name_on_doc` → "Whose name is printed on the document?"
- `course_or_subject_code` → "What course code or subject name appears on it?"
- `institution_name` → "What university or faculty name is printed on it?"

*Example: "Exam admission card for PMAT 21263 Linear Algebra, student name Kumara."*

---

### umbrella
- `umbrella_colour` → "What colour is the umbrella?"
- `pattern_or_brand` → "Does it have a pattern, logo, or brand name?"
- `handle_type` → "Describe the handle (wooden, curved, black grip)."
- `size_or_ribs` → "Is it a small foldable umbrella or a large one?"

*Example: "Navy blue foldable umbrella with a white Colombo University logo."*

---

### bottle
- `bottle_colour` → "What colour is the bottle?"
- `brand_or_sticker` → "What brand name or sticker is on it?"
- `liquid_inside` → "What liquid is inside, if visible?"
- `cap_colour_or_strap` → "What colour is the cap, or does it have a strap?"

*Example: "Green water bottle with a 'Stay Hydrated' sticker and a black strap."*

---

### glasses
- `frame_colour` → "What colour is the frame?"
- `frame_shape` → "What shape are the frames (round, rectangular, cat-eye)?"
- `brand_on_arm` → "What brand is printed on the arm?"
- `case_colour` → "What colour is the glasses case, if present?"

*Example: "Black rectangular glasses with a Ray-Ban logo on the arm."*

---

### other
- `item_description_detail` → "Describe one unique detail about the item."
- `owner_identifying_mark` → "What mark, label, or writing identifies the owner?"
- `item_location_hint` → "Where exactly was the item found that only the owner would know?"

*Example: "Scientific calculator with a small crack on the top-left corner and name 'Nimal' written inside the cover."*

---

## Tips for Person C

1. **Use details the finder can see but others cannot guess.** Avoid questions like "What colour is it?" if the colour is already in the public description.
2. **Prefer factual, specific answers.** "What hostel name is on the key tag?" is better than "What does the key tag say?"
3. **Keep it short.** One or two private detail fields are usually enough.
4. **Avoid sensitive data.** Do not use full NIC numbers, full passwords, or bank details.
5. **Sri Lankan university examples help.** Mentioning faculty names, hostel names, or course codes makes questions feel natural to students.
